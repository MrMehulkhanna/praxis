## The big correction: you are probably 2027 batch, not 2026

You said you're in **4th year, 7th semester** in September 2026. In Indian B.Tech, 7th sem runs
Jul–Dec of final year, so you graduate around **May–June 2027**. That makes you the **2027 batch**.

Why this matters enormously:

- **2026-batch mass hiring has already closed.** Cognizant's 2026 window closed July 2026. TCS NQT's
  2026 cycle ran Dec–Feb. Accenture's was Oct–Nov 2025. If you've been looking at those and feeling
  like you missed everything — you were looking at the wrong year.
- **The 2027-batch season is opening right now**, from Aug–Sep 2026 onward. Cognizant's 2027 cycle
  is reported opening in exactly this window.

**You are not late. Your season is just starting.** That's the single most useful thing I found
tonight. Verify your own expected graduation month and use it on every application.

(Your "job by December" target is likely TIET's *on-campus* placement season, which runs during
7th–8th sem. Off-campus runs in parallel — do both.)

## How to actually work this
1. **Register on the portals first** (TCS iON, Cognizant, Accenture). Registration is the bottleneck,
   not applying — do it once, then applications take ~20 min each.
2. **Apply while learning.** Your mentor was right: "you apply while learning" — waiting until you
   feel ready is what kills freshers. TCS NQT is a test you can retake every few weeks; each attempt
   is free practice.
3. **Track everything**: `desk career add "Company" "Role" -u URL --applied`, then
   `desk career status <n> applied`.
4. **CGPA**: yours (~6.5–7) clears most service-company cutoffs (usually 6.0 or 6.5). It will block
   some product companies. Off-campus + rolling ATS is exactly the right strategy for your profile.
